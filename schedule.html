Itinerary

Two days.

Debate runs across two days, 28th and 29th November 2026. Registration opens at seven in the morning on Day 1, and the closing ceremony ends at seven in the evening on Day 2 — with seven council sessions in between.

This is the working itinerary. Timings may shift slightly before the conference; the final version is issued with your allocation. Conference dates: To be announced.

What the blocks mean

If some of that vocabulary is new, here is what actually happens.

Roll call

The chair reads out every country in the council. You answer "present" or "present and voting" — the second commits you to taking a side on every substantive vote.

Opening speeches

Each delegation gets the floor briefly to state its national position on the agenda. It sets the terms for everything that follows.

Council session

Formal debate: speeches from the speakers list, moderated caucuses on specific sub-topics, and unmoderated caucuses for open negotiation.

Lobbying and merging

Blocs form, draft resolutions get written, and competing drafts are merged into something that can survive a vote. This is where most of the real work happens.

Opening ceremony

The whole conference in one auditorium — the Secretariat, the faculty and the chief guest set the tone before councils break out.

Closing ceremony

Resolutions reported, and awards announced: Best Delegate, Best Speaker, Most Likely to End Up in the UN, and two Verbal Mentions in every council.

Who runs this

Our team.

LSCMUN is organised by students of the Department of Life Sciences at Christ University, Bangalore, under faculty supervision. The Secretariat sets the academic direction; the Organising Committee makes it happen.

Core Secretariat

The team holding the whole conference together.

Organising Committee

Eight teams, each owning one part of the conference.

Each team is led by a head who sits on the organising board and reports to the Secretary General.

In every council

The four roles inside the room.

Chairperson

In charge of the overall supervision of the council they are appointed to. Before the conference, chairs correct position papers and assist delegates with preparation. During it, they chair and supervise every session and keep it running to time. Two per council, one for the Security Council.

Delegate

A participant representing a country other than their own, deliberating from that country's perspective rather than their own. For two days you are an accredited diplomat of the nation you represent, bound by the Rules of Procedure. 20–30 in each council.

Security

Stationed in the council to make sure proceedings run smoothly and no misbehaviour occurs, and to accompany any delegate who needs to leave the conference room for any reason. One per council.

Runner

Also stationed in each council. Delivers the diplomatic notes delegates pass around and reads them to ensure proper decorum and diplomacy is maintained in what gets sent. One per council.
